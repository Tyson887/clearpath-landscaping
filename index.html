Questions.

    

      
Do you take away the old grass and soil?+
Yes. We dig out the existing lawn, roots and topsoil and take it all away. No skip bin needed.

      
Why choose large format pavers?+
They look clean and modern, have fewer joints for weeds to grow in, and make a yard feel bigger. They need a very flat base, which is exactly what we build.

      
Can you fix a bumpy or sloping lawn?+
Yes. We strip the old grass, cut down the high spots, fill the low ones and grade the ground with string lines so it drains properly. Then fresh underlay and new turf go down on a dead-level base.

      
How are stepping pavers set into lawn?+
Each paver is dug in, bedded on a firm base and set flush with the grass so you can mow straight over the top. We space them to a comfortable walking stride.

      
How long does a yard revamp take?+
Most backyards take a few days from excavation to clean-up. We'll give you a firm timeline in your written quote.

      
Where do you work?+
All of Greater Sydney. We're based in the Hills District and travel right across the city.
